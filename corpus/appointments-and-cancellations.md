# Appointments and cancellations

## Opening hours
Both branches are open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 8:00 pm. On Sundays only the Indiranagar branch is open, from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm, for emergencies and pre-booked appointments.

## Branches
The Indiranagar branch is on 12th Main Road, HAL 2nd Stage, Bengaluru. The Koramangala branch is on 80 Feet Road, 5th Block, Bengaluru. Both branches have wheelchair access. Parking is available only at the Koramangala branch.

## Booking an appointment
Appointments can be booked on WhatsApp, by phone, or at the front desk. Booked patients receive a WhatsApp reminder 24 hours before the visit. Please arrive 10 minutes early for a first visit to fill in the medical history form.

## Cancellations and rescheduling
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 12 hours before the appointment. Cancelling with less than 12 hours' notice, or missing an appointment, costs ₹300, which is adjusted against your next visit. After two missed appointments in six months, future bookings need a ₹500 advance.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the appointment may be shortened or moved to the next free slot on the same day.

## Dental emergencies
For severe pain, swelling or a knocked-out tooth, call the emergency line. A dentist is on call until 10:00 pm every day. A knocked-out adult tooth has the best chance of being saved if you reach the clinic within 60 minutes; keep the tooth in milk on the way.
