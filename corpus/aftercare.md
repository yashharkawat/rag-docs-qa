# Aftercare instructions

## After a tooth extraction
Bite on the gauze for 45 minutes. Do not rinse, spit forcefully or drink through a straw for 24 hours, so the blood clot stays in place. Eat soft, cool food on the first day. Light bleeding for a few hours is normal; call the clinic if bleeding is still heavy after 24 hours.

## After a root canal
The tooth may feel tender for 2 to 3 days. Avoid chewing hard food on that side until the permanent crown is fitted. Take painkillers only as prescribed by your dentist.

## After scaling and polishing
Some sensitivity to cold is normal for a few days. Use a sensitivity toothpaste and avoid very hot or very cold drinks for 48 hours.

## After teeth whitening
For 48 hours, avoid tea, coffee, red wine, turmeric-heavy curries and smoking, because freshly whitened teeth stain easily.

## When to call the clinic
Call the clinic if you have a fever, swelling that gets worse after the third day, or pain that the prescribed medicine does not control.
