# Services and prices

## General dentistry
A routine check-up and consultation costs ₹500. It includes an examination by a dentist and a written treatment plan. X-rays are charged separately.

A digital X-ray of a single tooth costs ₹300. A full-mouth OPG X-ray costs ₹900.

Scaling and polishing (cleaning) costs ₹1,500 per session. Most patients need one session; heavy tartar may need two.

## Fillings and root canals
A composite (tooth-coloured) filling costs ₹1,800 per tooth. A glass ionomer filling for children costs ₹1,200.

Root canal treatment costs ₹6,500 for a front tooth and ₹8,500 for a molar. The crown is not included. Most root canals are completed in two visits.

## Crowns and implants
A metal-ceramic crown costs ₹7,000. A zirconia crown costs ₹14,000.

A single dental implant with crown starts from ₹38,000. The price includes the implant, abutment and crown. Bone grafting, if needed, is quoted separately after a CBCT scan, which costs ₹2,500.

## Orthodontics
Metal braces cost ₹35,000 for the full course. Clear aligners start from ₹95,000, depending on the number of trays.

Orthodontic consultations are free for patients under 18.

## Cosmetic dentistry
In-clinic teeth whitening costs ₹12,000 per session. A take-home whitening kit costs ₹6,000.
