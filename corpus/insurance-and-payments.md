# Insurance and payments

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, debit cards, credit cards and cash. Cheques are not accepted.

## EMI options
No-cost EMI is available on treatments above ₹20,000 through partner banks, over 3, 6 or 9 months. EMI is not available for cosmetic teeth whitening.

## Insurance
BrightSmile is a cashless partner of Star Health and HDFC ERGO for accidental dental injuries only. Most health insurance policies in India do not cover routine dental treatment. For those treatments we provide an itemised invoice that you can submit to your insurer for reimbursement.

## Corporate plans
Employees of partner companies get 10% off all treatments except implants and aligners. Bring your company ID card to your first visit.

## Refunds
Advance payments for aligners are refundable only until the aligner trays are ordered. Once the trays are manufactured, the advance is non-refundable. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days.
